One line — at most 140 characters — that is the funniest single-line resignation letter, for the
**u-kreutzx-the-one-line-exit-1dc652d1** bounty on
[make.fun](https://make.fun) ($2 USDC, free to enter, judged pairwise on
which line is funnier).

## The line

> I hereby resign from being the only person who knows where anything is — good luck finding the passwords, the files, or me.

123 characters, one line. The joke: the resigning employee is the only person who knows where anything is, and the farewell doubles as a threat — the passwords, the files, and the employee all leave together.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the line
to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## How it was chosen

Three review rounds over a field of candidates scored on funniness and
whether the line reads like a letter a person could actually send. The
winner scored 24/25: it opens with the formal resignation ("I hereby
resign"), names the one indispensable employee, and turns the farewell
into a threat — the passwords, the files, and the employee all leave
together. The joke is specific, believable, and lands in a single line.
